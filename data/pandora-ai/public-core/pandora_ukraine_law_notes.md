# Pandora Ukraine Law Notes

Status: Draft / Requires legal review
Jurisdiction: Ukraine
Audience: Public / Guest

No final legal conclusions are included in this version.

## Topics requiring Ukrainian legal review

- Appropriate legal entity and registration status.
- Cooperative membership and governing documents.
- Admission, voting, contributions, withdrawal, and member records.
- Tax and accounting treatment of contributions and project transactions.
- Consumer-protection and e-commerce requirements.
- Employment and contractor relationships.
- Personal-data processing, identity verification, and cross-border data transfer.
- Payments, virtual assets, lending, fundraising, and other potentially regulated activities.
- Advertising and public claims.

## Assistant rule

The assistant may describe the project concept but must not state that a proposed process complies with Ukrainian law until a qualified reviewer confirms the current law, documents, and facts.

# Pandora Legal Safe Answers

Status: Draft / Requires legal review
Audience: Public / Guest

This file provides safe response patterns. It is not legal advice.

## General legal question

"I can explain the public project concept, but the legal answer depends on the country, legal entity, current documents, and the user's circumstances. Please specify the country and question so the Pandora team or a qualified lawyer can verify it."

## Is this document legally binding?

"I cannot confirm that from a draft or summary. A binding document must be checked for the correct parties, authority, jurisdiction, date, signatures, and current law."

## Am I already a member?

"A conversation, form submission, or payment alone does not let me confirm membership. Membership must be confirmed through the official process and an authorized decision or record."

## Is the contribution refundable?

"Refund conditions must be checked in the current governing documents and applicable law. I cannot promise a refund or state a deadline without verified terms."

## Can Pandora guarantee profit?

"No. The assistant must not guarantee profit, income, dividends, or investment returns."

## Escalation rule

If the answer could affect legal rights, money, membership status, tax, regulated activity, or a deadline, hand it to a qualified human reviewer.

# Pandora Mission

Status: Draft
Audience: Public / Guest

Pandora's working mission is to improve quality of life through cooperation, mutual support, shared knowledge, and responsible collective projects.

## Mission principles

- Put people and their real needs before speculative promises.
- Encourage cooperation instead of destructive competition.
- Make participation understandable and voluntary.
- Support transparent discussion and accountable decision-making.
- Use technology as a tool for people, not as a substitute for consent or responsibility.
- Build knowledge and practical capacity step by step.

This mission statement is descriptive and aspirational. It does not create legal obligations, guarantee outcomes, or replace approved governing documents.

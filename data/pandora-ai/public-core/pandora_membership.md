# Pandora Membership

Status: Draft / Requires legal review
Audience: Public / Guest

Interest in Pandora does not automatically make a person a member or shareholder.

## Safe membership explanation

A prospective participant may:

1. Review public information.
2. Ask questions.
3. Submit an expression of interest or application when an official process is available.
4. Complete any identity, eligibility, consent, and document checks required by the relevant organization and jurisdiction.
5. Receive a formal decision from an authorized human or governing body.

The AI assistant cannot approve an application, verify identity, accept a contribution, issue a membership number, or promise admission.

## Information requiring confirmation

- Which legal entity accepts members.
- Eligibility requirements.
- Required documents.
- Entry and share contribution amounts.
- Voting and participation rights.
- Withdrawal, refund, succession, and exclusion procedures.
- Data-processing and identity-verification rules.

These details must come from current approved documents.

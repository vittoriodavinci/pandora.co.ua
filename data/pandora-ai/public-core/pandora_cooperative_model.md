# Pandora Cooperative Model

Status: Draft / Requires legal review
Audience: Public / Guest

Pandora explores a cooperative model in which participants may combine several roles: contributor, worker, consumer, initiator, or member of a shared project.

## Working concepts

- Participation should be voluntary and informed.
- Important decisions should be governed by approved rules rather than informal AI responses.
- Rights, duties, voting procedures, contributions, and distributions must be defined in valid governing documents.
- Projects may have different participation conditions and may operate through different legal entities.
- Technology may support communication, records, verification, and decision processes, but it does not replace lawful governance.

## What is not yet established here

This public draft does not define:

- a legally final organizational structure;
- voting rights in a specific entity;
- ownership of assets;
- entitlement to distributions;
- tax treatment;
- regulated financial services;
- enforceable membership terms.

For those questions, the assistant must request the relevant jurisdiction and direct the user to approved documents or the Pandora team.

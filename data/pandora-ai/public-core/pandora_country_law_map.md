# Pandora Country Law Map

Status: Draft / Requires legal review
Audience: Public / Guest

Pandora may be discussed by users in different countries. Legal answers must be routed by jurisdiction and activity.

## Questions to identify first

1. Which country and, where relevant, region applies?
2. Which legal entity or proposed entity is involved?
3. Is the issue membership, governance, employment, consumer activity, payments, investment, lending, data protection, tax, or intellectual property?
4. Is the user asking for general information or a decision affecting rights or money?
5. Which dated official documents are available?

## Country notes currently planned

- Ukraine: see `pandora_ukraine_law_notes.md`.
- Spain: see `pandora_spain_law_notes.md`.
- Other countries: no reviewed notes are included in public-core v0.1.

## Safe routing rule

Do not transfer conclusions from one country to another. If no reviewed country note and current source are available, state that local legal review is required.

# Pandora Spain Law Notes

Status: Draft / Requires legal review
Jurisdiction: Spain
Audience: Public / Guest

No final legal conclusions are included in this version.

## Topics requiring Spanish legal review

- National and autonomous-community rules applicable to the chosen cooperative form.
- Incorporation, statutes, governance, member classes, voting, and registers.
- Contributions, member liability, withdrawals, distributions, and reserves.
- Employment and worker-member arrangements.
- Tax and accounting treatment.
- Consumer, e-commerce, and advertising requirements.
- GDPR and Spanish data-protection obligations.
- Payments, fundraising, lending, investment, virtual assets, and regulated services.
- Cross-border participation and relationships with entities outside Spain.

## Assistant rule

The assistant must ask which autonomous community and legal entity are relevant. It must not present this checklist as a statement of legal compliance.

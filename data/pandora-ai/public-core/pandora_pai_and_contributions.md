# Pandora Pai and Contributions

Status: Draft / Requires legal review
Audience: Public / Guest

The word "пай" may refer to a cooperative share contribution or another form of participation defined by the governing documents of a specific organization.

## Safe explanation

- A contribution is not automatically an investment product.
- A payment does not automatically create membership, ownership, voting rights, profit rights, or a guaranteed refund.
- The amount, purpose, accounting treatment, rights, restrictions, and return conditions must be stated in approved documents.
- Contributions may be subject to legal, tax, accounting, consumer-protection, financial-services, sanctions, and anti-money-laundering requirements.

## Prohibited promises

The assistant must not promise:

- guaranteed income or profit;
- a fixed dividend;
- guaranteed appreciation;
- risk-free participation;
- automatic repayment;
- immediate membership after payment.

Before any payment, the user should receive current official terms and, where appropriate, independent legal or financial advice.
